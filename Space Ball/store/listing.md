## English (en-US)

### Short description

Offline ball jump game: bounce across neon space planets and collect the stars!

### Full description

Space Ball is an offline ball jump game set in neon space. Bounce from platform to platform, dodge deadly traps and reach the warp gate on 60 levels across 4 planets. No internet needed!

★ 60 LEVELS ON 4 SPACE PLANETS
• Lumina: learn to jump and ride moving platforms
• Verdis: bounce on trampolines and slide across slime
• Asteria: dodge blinking lasers, falling asteroids and crumbling platforms
• Nebulon: float in low gravity, warp through portals and escape black holes

★ EASY TO PLAY, HARD TO MASTER
Tap to jump, tap again in the air for a double jump and steer your ball left or right. A quick tutorial gets you started, and each new obstacle is introduced step by step. This arcade platformer rewards timing, not luck.

★ COLLECT THE STARS
Every level hides 3 stars. Reach the gate to finish, grab every star for a perfect run and climb the Google Play Games leaderboard.

★ NEON BALL SKINS
Spend stars in the shop and unlock glowing skins for your ball: Nebula, Verdant, Magma, Crystal, Gold and the rainbow Prism.

★ DAILY CHALLENGE
A new space level every day, with bonus stars for beating it.

★ PLAY YOUR WAY, ANYWHERE
Stuck on a level? Try one of the next ones and come back later. A new planet opens after you complete 10 levels of the previous one. Play offline on a plane, on the subway or anywhere without Wi-Fi.

Colorful space landscapes, glowing effects and satisfying bounce sounds make every jump feel great. If you like ball games, jumping games and space arcade games, Space Ball is made for you. Get it now and bounce your way to the stars!
